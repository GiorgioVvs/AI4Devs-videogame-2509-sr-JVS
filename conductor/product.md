# Initial Concept
A very basic "snake" variant where you control a worm that comes jumps  out to the surface to eat different kinds of pray and each time it eats it gets bigger

# Product Vision
Worminator is a dynamic twist on the classic "Snake" mechanic. Players control a worm that must surface to consume various types of prey. The core gameplay loop focuses on growth and survival, with different prey types adding layers of strategy and risk-reward decision-making.

# Core Mechanics
- **Surface Action:** The worm jumps to the surface to catch prey, creating a rhythmic and engaging movement pattern.
- **Growth System:** Consuming prey increases the worm's length and score, increasing the difficulty as the worm becomes harder to maneuver.

# Prey Types
- **Common Prey:** Regular food sources that provide steady growth and score increases.
- **Elite Prey:** Rare, high-value targets that offer significant point boosts and accelerated growth.
- **Cursed Prey:** Dangerous food items that offer high rewards but come with negative status effects, such as shrinking the worm or temporarily reversing controls.
